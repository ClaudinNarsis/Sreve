## ✍️ Writing Guidelines: Social Media Content

### Structure (Every Blog Post):
1. **Hook** – Relatable pain point or trending insight (first 2 sentences)
2. **Context** – Why this matters now (2025 trends, algorithm changes, creator burnout)
3. **Ideas/Solutions** – Numbered lists, specific examples, actionable steps
4. **Human Touch** – Real creator stories, behind-the-scenes, authentic struggles
5. **Tool Integration** – Natural CTAs to Sreve tools (not forced)
6. **Social Proof** – Results, case studies, testimonials where possible

### Tone Principles:
- **Conversational**: Write like texting a fellow creator
- **Specific**: "47 ideas" not "lots of ideas"
- **Opinionated**: Take a stance (most content is boring, AI misses emotion, etc.)
- **Trend-aware**: Reference current platform changes, viral formats
- **Authentic**: Share what works AND what doesn't

### SEO Best Practices:
- **Primary keyword in H1** (only one H1 per page)
- **Secondary keywords in H2s** (5-8 H2 sections)
- **Long-tail variations in H3s** (2-4 per H2)
- **Internal links**: Minimum 8-12 per post
  - 3-4 tool links
  - 4-6 related blog posts
  - 2-3 main pages
- **FAQ section**: Required (3-5 questions minimum)
- **Meta description**: 150-160 chars with primary keyword
- **Schema markup**: Article + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList

### Content Length by Type:
- **Pillar posts** (ultimate guides): 2,500-3,500 words
- **List posts** (177 ideas, 50 ideas): 1,800-2,500 words
- **How-to guides**: 1,500-2,000 words
- **Comparison posts**: 1,800-2,200 words
- **Trend reports**: 1,200-1,800 words

### Internal Linking Strategy:
**Every post must link to:**
- 2-3 posts in same content pillar (cluster)
- 1-2 posts in related pillars
- 3-4 relevant Sreve tools
- 1-2 main pages (pricing, features, homepage)

## 🎨 Content Formatting Standards

### Required Elements:
1. **Compelling H1** with primary keyword (max 60 chars)
2. **Lead paragraph** with `.lead` class (2-3 sentences, hook reader)
3. **Jump links/Table of Contents** for posts >2,000 words
4. **Numbered/bulleted lists** for scannability
5. **Bold key phrases** for emphasis
6. **CTA boxes** (2-3 per article, orange background)
7. **Comparison tables** where relevant
8. **Real examples/screenshots** (not stock images)
9. **FAQ section** (required for schema)
10. **Final CTA** with trust signals

### Visual Hierarchy:
- **H1**: Primary keyword, article title
- **H2**: Main sections (5-8 per post)
- **H3**: Subsections (2-4 per H2)
- **Bold**: Key phrases, important points
- **Lists**: Break up long paragraphs
- **CTA boxes**: Orange background, clear action
- **Blockquotes**: Testimonials, notable quotes

## 🎓 Learning from Top-Performing Content

### What Works (Based on 2025 Trends):
1. **Specific numbers**: "177 ideas" > "lots of ideas"
2. **Real examples**: Screenshots, case studies, before/after
3. **Trend integration**: Current platform changes, viral formats
4. **Platform-specific**: Instagram ≠ TikTok ≠ LinkedIn strategies

4. ❌ Platform-agnostic advice (one-size-fits-all)
5. ❌ AI hype without substance
6. ❌ Thin content (<1,000 words)
7. ❌ No internal links or CTAs
